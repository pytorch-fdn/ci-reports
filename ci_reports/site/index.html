PyTorch Foundation CI Reports

Combined total (all owning accounts: LF, Meta, AMD) per month. Click a bar or point to open that month's detailed report.

This breakdown covers instance-hours only, matching the sheet's own Architecture pivot -- usage billed in other units (data transfer, storage, requests, etc.) isn't attributable to an architecture and is excluded here, so the bars sum to less than the Runtime total shown in each month's report.

Months are snapshotted once and then treated as immutable, so this trend is stable across rebuilds. A runner type or instance family not yet in the mapping tables never excludes a month -- its cost, when known, is shown under an explicit "N/A" architecture in that month's detailed report instead of being hidden.
